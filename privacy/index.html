Privacy Policy

Orlando Oracle Inc. · Last updated 2026-08-22

This policy explains how Orlando Oracle Inc. ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our SMS operations-assistant platform.

Information we collect

When you opt in, we collect your mobile phone number and the content of the messages you send to the platform, solely to provide the operational assistant service you request.

How we use it

We use your phone number and message content only to operate the assistant and deliver the messages you request (confirmations, reminders, status updates, and operational responses). We do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell or share mobile information, opt-in data, or SMS consent with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in and consent information is never shared with any third parties. We may use service providers (such as our SMS carrier) strictly to deliver the messages you request.

Opt-out

You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data retention & security

We retain message data only as needed to provide the service and apply reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@orlandooracle.com.
